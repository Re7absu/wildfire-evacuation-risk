Limitations

  • The data is small: — fires in total, so every score has some uncertainty.
  • The model depends almost only on distance and is weaker at predicting when a fire hits.
  • The 72 hour risk is about 98% for every fire. That is a side effect of the scoring rule, so use the 12, 24 and 48 hour risks for decisions.
  • No weather, wind or terrain data was available. Adding it is the best next step.
What the scores mean
  • Hybrid score: the competition score, 0.3 × C-index + 0.7 × (1 − Brier). Higher is better.
  • C-index: how often the model ranks the more urgent of two fires first.
  • Brier score: the average squared gap between the predicted chance and what happened (0 = perfect).
  • Cross-validation: the training fires are split into 5 parts; each part is scored by a model trained on the other 4.
