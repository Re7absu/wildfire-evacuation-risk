Read with care

Limitations

  • Small data: — fires in total, so every score has some uncertainty.
  • Weaker at predicting when a fire hits than whether it does.
  • The 72 hour risk is about 98% for every fire, a side effect of the scoring rule. Use 12, 24 and 48 hours for decisions.
  • No weather, wind or terrain data yet. Adding it is the best next step.
What the scores mean
  • Hybrid score: 0.3 × C-index + 0.7 × (1 − weighted Brier). Higher is better.
  • C-index: how often the more urgent of two fires is ranked first.
  • Brier score: average squared gap between predicted chance and what happened (0 is perfect).
  • Cross-validation: the training fires are split into 5 parts; each part is scored by a model trained on the other 4.
